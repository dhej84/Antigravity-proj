Structured Learning

Your 8-Week Journey to Design Mastery

A proven, structured path from zero to portfolio-ready. Follow at your own pace — every week builds on the last.

Week 1–2
Design Foundations & Figma Basics
Learn design principles, color theory, typography, and Figma workspace setup. Complete your first UI screen.
Week 3–4
Wireframing, Flows & Prototyping
Build full user flows, interactive prototypes, and navigation systems. Learn component-based design.
Week 5–6
Real Project — App/Website Design
Design a complete mobile app or website UI from scratch. Get peer reviews and iterate on feedback.
Week 7
Web Dev Basics + AI Coding Tools
Convert your Figma designs to code with HTML/CSS. Use GitHub Copilot and v0.dev to accelerate.
Week 8
Portfolio, Case Studies & Launch
Write compelling case studies, publish your portfolio site, and prepare for client outreach or job applications.
